About Us

Closets Built Around Your Space, Your Style and Your Routine

Manhattan Custom Closets And Wardrobes is a cabinet maker serving homeowners, renters and designers across New York City. We take detailed measurements, review your storage needs and craft closet cabinets that make full use of every wall, corner and ceiling line.

Choose a contemporary closet, a built-in wardrobe, a modular system or a luxury walk-in with custom organizers. We handle the design, fabrication and installation so your storage looks refined and works every day.
